Voice

Casual by Nature began as ink on paper — a personal handwriting style developed over years of note-taking, sketching, and cycling logs. Each letterform is the real thing: drawn once, with a pen, and then carefully engineered into a professional typeface.

Four weights give you the same expressive hand across a range of uses — from bold display headlines to fine-weight captions. 99 glyphs cover the full Latin alphabet, numerals, punctuation, currency symbols, and more.

This is not a font generated from a template. It's a voice.
